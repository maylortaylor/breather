# Context

Vocabulary for breather. Use these terms, not synonyms. Source: [`docs/PLAN.md`](docs/PLAN.md).

- **Phase**: one segment of the 4-7-8 breath: inhale (4), hold (7) or exhale (8).
- **Cycle**: one full pass through the three phases, 19 beats.
- **Beat**: the unit of breath timing; its length comes from the tempo slider, or from fixed seconds in seconds mode.
- **Detent**: a snap point on a slider; tempo has one at 60 BPM, the metronome slider has two.
- **Layer**: one independently mixed sound source (noise, ocean, bubbles, whales, music, metronome) with its own volume.
- **Preset**: a saved set of levels, tempo, metronome level, sound choice, music mood and dim mode.
- **Theme**: a background look driven by the breath; the first is the aurora borealis.
- **Listening Lab**: a plain audition page with one play button and one slider per sound, used to approve or reject each synthesized sound before the main UI exists.
